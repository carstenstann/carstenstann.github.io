Welcome to my personal site, where I write about topics that interest me, review books I've read or write down thoughts about them, and share my projects. My interests vary widely and with time, but are primarily centered around Data Science and Economics.

It's been a while since I've attempted a personal site, but I've decided to give it another go. Setting it up couldn't have been easier thanks to the amazing work of behind the open-source [Hugo framework](https://gohugo.io/), this sleek [hello-friend-ng](https://github.com/rhazdon/hugo-theme-hello-friend-ng) theme developed by [Djordje Atlialp](https://atlialp.com/), and the [gallery shortcode](https://github.com/mfg92/hugo-shortcode-gallery) by [Matze](https://matze.rocks/about/) that presents my photography portfolio.

With that, I'll run the following command and hope to see you again on down the line.

```bash
$ hugo new site ~/carstenstann.github.io
```
